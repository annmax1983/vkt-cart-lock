1. Summary

The short version: the cart, the countdown, your messages and your statistics live in your browser. The extension has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only network requests it makes are for optional Premium license activation, validation and deactivation.

VKT Cart Lock has a single purpose: to prevent a checkout from being completed until a timer chosen by the user has ended. Everything the extension does serves that purpose.

2. Data kept on your device

The following records are stored locally through chrome.storage.local. They are not uploaded by the extension:

  • Settings: cooldown length, scope, timer start, sound choice, quiet hours, message mode, custom messages, allowlist, force-lock sites, small-order exemption, password settings and display preferences.
  • Timers: the site or product key, start and expiry times, blocked-attempt count, cart total and currency, item count, and whether the hold was already recorded.
  • Statistics: site hostnames where a checkout was paused, held amounts grouped by currency, monthly totals, emotion tags chosen at the lock screen, outcomes (held or purchased) and timestamps.
  • Self-check notes: the hostname, time and reason when the extension checks whether its checkout detection still works on a site.
  • Settings PIN: a random salt and a salted SHA-256 hash. The PIN itself and the hash never leave the device and are never written to sync storage.
  • Premium license record: license key, a random installation ID, plan key, status, activation date and expiry date.

Cart totals and item counts are read from the shop page in your browser only when a checkout attempt is intercepted. They are used to show the held amount and the small-order exemption. Page content is not transmitted.

3. Cross-device sync (Premium)

Cross-device sync is off by default. When a Premium user turns it on, the extension writes a slim copy of the following to chrome.storage.sync:

  • settings and custom messages (the settings PIN is removed);
  • active timer records, without statistics or history.

This data is handled by your browser vendor's sync service and is subject to that provider's privacy policy. The extension does not operate the sync service and cannot read other extensions' or other users' synced data. Turning sync off clears the extension's sync records.

Local-only data such as held amounts, monthly totals, emotion statistics and history is not part of sync. It stays on the device where it was recorded.

4. License activation

Premium is optional. When you activate, validate or deactivate a license, the extension sends a request to https://api.annmax1983.com/api/license. The request contains:

FieldPurpose
License keyIdentify the purchased license
Random installation IDBind the license to a device; generated locally, not derived from hardware
Plugin ID (cartlock)Confirm which product the license covers
Browser name and versionTroubleshoot activation failures
Browser language(s) and timezoneTroubleshoot activation failures and localize support
User agent stringTroubleshoot activation failures

The license server uses this information solely to activate, validate and deactivate licenses and to prevent license abuse. Validation happens at most every two hours while the browser is running; if the server is unreachable, an already-activated Premium license keeps working offline from the local record.

No browsing data, cart contents, messages or statistics are included in these requests.

5. What is never collected

  • No browsing history or list of visited pages.
  • No page content, form values, search terms or product details sent to a server.
  • No payment card numbers, CVV codes, billing addresses or payment-provider credentials. Payment-provider hosts and frames are explicitly excluded from the guard.
  • No keystroke logging, mouse tracking, screen recording or session replay.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics events or crash reports.
  • No account, email address or profile is created by the extension.

6. How data is used

Local and synced data is used only to provide the features you can see: keeping a timer, showing the lock screen, applying site rules, producing the statistics dashboard and restoring settings on another device.

License data is used only to determine whether Premium features are enabled. The extension does not profile users, personalize ads, score creditworthiness or make automated decisions with legal effects.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal data with advertisers, data brokers or unrelated third parties. Data is disclosed only:

  • to the license API described above, when you choose to use Premium;
  • to your browser vendor's sync service, when you choose to enable sync;
  • if required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

VKT Cart Lock's use of information received from Chrome APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until the extension's storage is removed. To delete data:

  • turn off Premium sync first, which clears the extension's synced records;
  • deactivate a Premium license to remove its device binding on the server;
  • uninstall the extension, which removes its local storage.

Server-side license records are kept while the license is active and for a reasonable period afterward to handle renewals, refunds and abuse prevention. To request deletion of a license record, contact us through the website below and include the license key or installation ID.

9. Security

License requests use HTTPS. The settings PIN is stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text. Access to extension storage is limited to the extension's own pages and service worker by the browser's extension security model. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but the extension deliberately collects as little as possible so that a breach would expose as little as possible.

10. Children

VKT Cart Lock is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The extension does not require an account and does not ask for age or identity.

11. Policy changes

If a future version changes what the extension stores or transmits, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change. Material changes will be noted on the product website or in the extension's store listing.
