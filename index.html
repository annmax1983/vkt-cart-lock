01

A pause that actually holds

Most impulse-buying tools leave the door open: snooze, skip, unlock early. VKT Cart Lock does not.

01

Hard checkout lock: Clicks, checkout links, form submissions and checkout pages are intercepted before the order goes through.

02

The timer lives outside the tab: Close the page, restart the browser, come back tomorrow. Storage and alarms keep the lock running to the second.

03

Your words, not ours: Write up to 20 lines on Free, or 60 with Pro. Show them in fixed, rotating or random order.

04

Sound or silence: Pick a thud, a chime or a soft cue — or turn sound off and let the countdown do the talking.

05

Rules for real life: Allowlist a grocery shop, force the lock on a small store, exempt essentials under a set amount, and extend waiting during major sales.

06

See what the pause held: A local ledger counts every blocked checkout, the money held back and the mood you tagged at the moment.

07

End-of-lock check-in: With notifications on, one notice asks whether you bought it or let it go, and the answer lands in the ledger.

02

The free tier is a complete lock

Not a trial. Pro adds longer waits, finer control and deeper reporting for people who want them.

Free

24 store presets + generic matching, 1, 6, 24 or 72-hour cooldowns, 20 custom messages, three sounds, Allowlist, force-lock sites and small-order exemption, Emotion tags, ledger, toolbar countdown and the end-of-lock check-in.

Pro

Custom cooldowns from 10 minutes to 30 days, Per-product timers, or start when an item enters the cart, Up to 60 messages, quiet hours and confirm after expiry, Settings and timers sync through your browser account, 12-month trend, site ranking and emotion reports (your bought / let-go answers stay in the free ledger and in the CSV export), CSV export and full JSON backup.

The waiting rules stay the same on both plans: no skip, no snooze, no early unlock. Uninstalling the extension remains the only escape. No account is required for the Free tier. Uninstalling removes local data.

Your cart is nobody else's data

Timers, messages and statistics stay in your browser. The guard reads checkout controls and cart totals locally to decide whether to block. It never transmits page content and never hooks network requests.
